Around Cedar Nest

Places to visit
in Sainj Valley

Sainj is Himachal's best-kept secret. Cedar Nest puts you at its heart — minutes from ancient meadows, hidden waterfalls and a UNESCO sanctuary.

01

Sacred Meadow · 2200m

Shangarh
Meadow

One of the most breathtaking alpine meadows in all of Himachal, surrounded by towering deodar cedars with a centuries-old wooden temple at its heart. Guests wake early for sunrise here, when mist clings to the cedars and the Great Himalayan National Park ridge rises dramatically behind.

12 km from Cedar Nest · 35 min drive

02

Hidden Cascade

Rupi Raila
Waterfall

Hidden deep in a mossy gorge carved by millennia of glacial melt, Rupi Raila is the waterfall locals rarely share with outsiders. The walk through forest and boulder-strewn stream beds is itself a meditation. Cedar Nest's guides know the trail intimately.

8 km from Cedar Nest · 25 min drive + 45 min trek

03

UNESCO World Heritage Buffer Zone

Sainj Wildlife
Sanctuary

A UNESCO World Heritage buffer zone home to the Himalayan tahr, snow leopard, brown bear, and over 220 bird species. Enter on foot directly from the Cedar Nest property — the wilderness begins at your doorstep.

Adjacent to Cedar Nest · Enter on foot
